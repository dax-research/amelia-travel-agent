# ✈️ Amelia

### Autonomous AI Travel Concierge

> **Detect. Decide. Recover.**

Amelia is an autonomous AI travel concierge designed to handle flight disruptions in real time.

Instead of simply notifying travelers about a cancellation or missed connection, Amelia detects the disruption, evaluates alternatives, applies travel policies and customer preferences, coordinates flight and hotel changes, and keeps the traveler informed — with minimal or no manual intervention.

---

## 🚨 The Problem

Flight cancellations and missed connections create a stressful chain of manual tasks:

- Finding alternative flights
- Comparing prices and timings
- Checking travel policies
- Rebooking flights
- Modifying hotel reservations
- Managing additional costs
- Contacting customer support
- Keeping track of the updated itinerary

Traditional travel applications primarily **notify** travelers.

Amelia aims to **resolve the disruption**.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Amelia continuously monitors a traveler's itinerary and automatically responds when a disruption occurs.
